about

I’m a researcher and engineer. I’m currently a Principal Engineer at PsiQuantum, where most of my time goes to thinking about fault-tolerance, quantum error correction, architectures, and the role AI plays across all of them. I’ve also had a hand in simulation, compilation, and a wide variety of systems software as part of the effort to build a utility-scale quantum computer.

Most of my career has been focused on problems at the intersection of physics, software, hardware, and artificial intelligence. This has spanned a variety of applications and flavors including robotics, sensors, and, for the last seven years, quantum computing.

Much of my recent work lives at the AI corner of that intersection: neural decoders and reinforcement learning for fault-tolerant quantum computing, vision-language-action models for autonomous experimental science, autonomous agent systems for physics research, formalization and verification, and compiler toolchains grounded in the ZX-Calculus.
